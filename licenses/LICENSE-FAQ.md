## Community Edition

**Can I use it on my personal website or blog?**
Yes, if the site is personal and is not monetized (see below).

**What if my blog has ads, sponsors or affiliate links?**
Then the use is commercial and the Community License does not cover it. Get a commercial license, or remove the Software from that site.

**I have a "buy me a coffee" link.**
A generic voluntary donation link that does not unlock anything and is not a significant income does not alone make the use commercial. If unsure, ask (see "How do I ask for permission?").

**Can I use it on my portfolio?**
A personal portfolio showing your own learning and non-commercial projects, or used only to apply for a job, is fine. A portfolio or website that advertises your paid services, or that shows client work built with the Software, is commercial.

**Can I use it for a client project if the client does not pay me?**
Any work for another person or organization is not a personal project. Ask for permission first: the Doubt rule (Community License §3.5) says it is not allowed until confirmed.

**Can I use it at my employer, in an internal tool?**
No. Use in the business of a company or other organization needs a commercial license.

**I am a student. Can I use it for my homework or thesis?**
Yes, for coursework and personal learning projects that are not used commercially.

**I am a teacher. Can I use it in class?**
Yes, in instruction at an educational institution, including giving students the files together with the license. Not for the institution's public site or systems, and not for paid courses sold by an individual or company (those need permission or a commercial license).

**Can I modify the source code?**
Yes, for non-commercial use. The modified version stays under the Community License, keeps all notices and must say what you changed. Modifying the code does not give you any commercial right.

**Can I write my own plugin?**
Yes. Code you write for an independent plugin is yours. Running it with ABI7 Grid is still a use of ABI7 Grid, so a commercial use of the combination needs a commercial license.

**Can I publish my plugin?**
Yes, as long as it contains no ABI7 Grid code, and you say that ABI7 Grid must be licensed separately.

**Can I fork the repository on GitHub?**
Yes. Keep the license, notices and commercial-license notice; do not present the fork as official; do not relicense it.

**Can I publish a modified copy to npm or a CDN?**
Only free of charge, with the license and notices, and with package metadata that does not use a standard open-source license identifier. It must not be used to bypass the license.

**Can I put ABI7 Grid in a template, theme or course I sell?**
No (Community License §4 and §9).

**Is a non-profit organization automatically covered?**
No. Ask for written permission or get a commercial license.

**My hobby site became a business. What now?**
Before the change takes effect, stop using the Software or buy a commercial license (§6). There is no grace period, and a license purchased now does not cover earlier commercial use.

**How do I ask for permission?**
Write via https://abi7.com before you publish: your name, the project, whether any income, ads or clients are involved, and which parts of the Software you use. A permission is valid only in writing.

## Commercial licenses

**What is the difference between the two?**
*Single Developer ($29)* — one named developer, any number of that developer's projects, client projects and own commercial products. *Unlimited Developers ($199)* — one organization (with its subsidiaries) and all its developers and Projects.

**Is the license bound to a domain?**
No. The Single Developer License is bound to one developer, not to a domain or a project.

**Can I use the Single Developer License for my clients' websites?**
Yes, if you are the named developer and each client receives the finished project only. The client's own developers need their own license.

**Can my agency use one Single Developer License for the whole team?**
No. One license per developer, or the Unlimited Developers License for the agency itself.

**Do contractors count?**
Under the Unlimited Developers License a contractor may use the Software while working for the organization on its projects. They may not use it for other clients.

**Do subsidiaries and affiliates count?**
Subsidiaries (more than 50 % control) are included while the control lasts. Other affiliates are not, unless agreed in writing.

**Can I resell ABI7 Grid inside my own product?**
You may ship it embedded in a compiled project. You may not sell or distribute it as a library, or in a template, theme, UI kit, page builder or CMS plugin, without a separate written agreement.
