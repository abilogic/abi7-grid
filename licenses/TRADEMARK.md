"ABI7", "ABI7 Grid", "ABI7 Grid Community" and the associated logos and the domain abi7.com identify the product and services of Alexander Momot. This policy explains how the names may be used. It is separate from the copyright licenses.

## You may

- state truthfully that your project **uses** or **is compatible with** ABI7 Grid ("Built with ABI7 Grid", "Plugin for ABI7 Grid");
- link to https://github.com/abilogic/abi7-grid and https://abi7.com;
- use the names in articles, tutorials and documentation that describe the Software.

## You may not

- present a fork, a Modification or another product as the official ABI7 Grid, or use "ABI7" as the main part of its name (for example "ABI7 Grid Pro", "ABI7 Grid 2");
- suggest endorsement, partnership or sponsorship by the Licensor without written agreement;
- sell, advertise or offer commercial licenses of ABI7 Grid. **Commercial licenses are sold only at https://abi7.com;**
- register domains, social accounts, packages or trademarks that contain "ABI7" and could be confused with the official product;
- use the logos without permission.

## Forks and plugins

A fork should use a distinct name and say "based on ABI7 Grid by Alexander Momot". A plugin may be named "something for ABI7 Grid" but not "ABI7 something" in a way that suggests it is official. The Bundled Plugins use the `@abi7/` prefix only for official plugins in this repository; do not use that prefix for your own plugins.
