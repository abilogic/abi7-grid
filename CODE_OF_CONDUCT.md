# Code of Conduct

We want ABI7 Grid to be a respectful, welcoming project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith.
- Give and accept technical criticism politely; criticize ideas, not people.
- Keep discussions on topic; do not post spam or advertising.
- Respect the license terms of the project and of other people's work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats or doxxing.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Repeatedly ignoring a maintainer's request to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block users who violate this code. Report problems via https://abi7.com `[contact e-mail — TO BE COMPLETED]`. Reports are handled confidentially.
